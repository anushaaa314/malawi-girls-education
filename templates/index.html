Girls' Education in Malawi

Malawi is a country in Southeastern Africa with approximately 11 million women living in it. Recently, these women have gained more access to higher education, but social issues (such as period poverty, child marriges, pregnancies, and gender expectations) still remain in place.

Secondary Education Enrollment Rate in Malawi over Time

From 1990 to 2015, the secondary education rate for women in Malawi has grown by an impressive 400%. About a quarter of girls were enrolled in a form of secondary education as of 2015. Several organizations like CAMFED (Campaign for Female Education) Malawi, FAWEMA (Forum for African Women Educationalists - Malawi Chapter), and UNESCO have helped drive this change.

However, only 6.75% of girls went on to actually complete their secondary education in 2015.
